More Than Tea. It's a Moment.

Chai & Crumbs was born from a simple belief — that tea breaks are more than just drinks. They are moments to pause, talk, relax and enjoy something delicious.

Our Beginning

It started in 2019 with a tiny corner shop, one kettle, and a big dream. We wanted to recreate the feeling of home — the warmth of a chai made by someone who cares.

What began as a small neighbourhood chai stall grew into a beloved tea-time destination, thanks to every person who walked in, sat down, and shared a moment with us.

Our Tea

We source our tea leaves from small gardens across Assam, Darjeeling and the Nilgiris. Every blend is chosen for its character, aroma and the way it makes you feel.

From the robust masala chai to the delicate Kashmiri Kahwa, each cup is brewed with intention — never rushed, always warm.

Our Snacks

Our snacks are made in small batches throughout the day. Samosas with a perfect crisp, pakoras that are light and golden, puffs that crumble just right — these are the bites that make tea-time complete.

We follow traditional family recipes, prepared with fresh ingredients and a lot of love.

Our Promise

We promise to always serve you tea that is fresh, snacks that are warm, and a space where you feel at home.

Whether you come alone with a book, with friends for long conversations, or with family for an evening out — Chai & Crumbs is your place.

Tea is not just a drink. It is a moment.

Come share a cup with us.
